About Michelle

Who is Michelle Galoob?

What's the difference between a reading and the Intuition Mastery Program?

In a reading, Michelle receives guidance for you. The Intuition Mastery Program teaches you to hear, trust and use your own intuition — including how to access your own Akashic Records.

Readings

What is an Akashic Records reading?

The Akashic Records are described as a virtual library of thoughts and actions — past, present and future — where everyone has a file. In a reading, Michelle accesses your file to help you find your true potential, connect with your soul, and hear what your soul wants you to know.

What can I ask about in a reading?

Clients bring questions about areas of life that feel blocked, crossroads decisions, their soul's purpose, personal or business relationships, and wanting direction or validation on the path they're on. Business owners and investors also use readings for decision support.

How do readings work — in person or by phone?

Readings are phone sessions. You choose a length (30 minutes, 45 minutes or 1 hour, or a package of 2 or 4 one-hour sessions), book a time online, and Michelle calls you at your appointment.

How much does a reading cost?

A 30-minute reading is $200, 45 minutes is $285, and 1 hour is $375. Packages are $660 for two 1-hour sessions and $1,280 for four 1-hour sessions.

How should I prepare?

Come with your questions written down. As one client put it: take good notes and come to the session prepared with questions.

I'm skeptical of anything 'woo woo'. Is this for me?

Michelle describes herself as extremely level-headed and grounded — "No airy fairy here!" Her focus is practical: sharing what your soul wants you to know so you can move forward with clarity.

Which reading length should I choose?

If you have one or two focused questions, a 30- or 45-minute session can work well. For several areas of life — or your first reading — the 1-hour session gives the most room. Packages of 2 or 4 one-hour sessions suit ongoing guidance, for example around business decisions.

Can a reading help with business decisions?

Yes. Clients including investors and founders have described using Michelle's guidance for critical business decisions.

Intuition Mastery Program

What is the Intuition Mastery Program?

A program by Michelle Galoob that teaches you to hear your intuition clearly, trust it, and use it in daily life, relationships, work and decisions.

What will I learn?

The foundations of intuition, how to open your intuitive channels, your dominant intuitive language, how to tell your thoughts from your guidance, how to ask your spirit team the right questions and get yes/no answers, what the Akashic Records are and how to access your own, and grounding techniques.

What if I'm not naturally intuitive?

Everyone is intuitive — it's a built-in human capacity, not a special gift reserved for a few. Most people have simply been taught to ignore it. This program is specifically designed to help you reconnect with what's already there. If you've ever had a gut feeling, a hunch, or a moment where you 'just knew' — that's your intuition. We're going to strengthen it.

Do I need any prior experience with intuition or spiritual work?

Not at all. This program is designed to meet you exactly where you are — whether you're brand new to intuition development or have been exploring it for years. The foundations we build in the early modules create a solid base for everyone.

How long do I have access to the program?

Membership includes 12 months of live sessions and community access. The on-demand digital program is yours to keep and work through at your own pace.

What if I can't attend the live sessions?

All live sessions are recorded and made available to you. While attending live is recommended for the interactive experience, you won't miss any content.
