Program FAQs

What is the Intuition Mastery Program?

A program by Michelle Galoob that teaches you to hear your intuition clearly, trust it, and use it in daily life, relationships, work and decisions. Full details, pricing and enrollment are on michellegaloobtraining.com.

What will I learn?

The foundations of intuition, how to open your intuitive channels, your dominant intuitive language, how to tell your thoughts from your guidance, how to ask your spirit team the right questions and get yes/no answers, what the Akashic Records are and how to access your own, and grounding techniques.

No. The program is designed to meet you where you are, whether you're brand new to intuition or have been exploring it for years.
