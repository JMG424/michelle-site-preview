Frequently asked questions

What is an Akashic Records reading?

The Akashic Records are described as a virtual library of thoughts and actions — past, present and future — where everyone has a file. In a reading, Michelle accesses your file to help you find your true potential, connect with your soul, and hear what your soul wants you to know.

What can I ask about in a reading?

Clients bring questions about areas of life that feel blocked, crossroads decisions, their soul's purpose, personal or business relationships, and wanting direction or validation on the path they're on. Business owners and investors also use readings for decision support.

How do readings work — in person or by phone?

Readings are phone sessions. You choose a length (30 minutes, 45 minutes or 1 hour, or a package of 2 or 4 one-hour sessions), book a time online, and Michelle calls you at your appointment.

How much does a reading cost?

A 30-minute reading is $200, 45 minutes is $285, and 1 hour is $375. Packages are $660 for two 1-hour sessions and $1,280 for four 1-hour sessions.

How should I prepare?

Come with your questions written down. As one client put it: take good notes and come to the session prepared with questions.

I'm skeptical of anything 'woo woo'. Is this for me?

Michelle describes herself as extremely level-headed and grounded — "No airy fairy here!" Her focus is practical: sharing what your soul wants you to know so you can move forward with clarity.

What's the difference between a reading and the Intuition Mastery Program?

In a reading, Michelle receives guidance for you. The Intuition Mastery Program teaches you to hear, trust and use your own intuition — including how to access your own Akashic Records.
